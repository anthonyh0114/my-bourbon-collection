My Bourbon Collection - User's Guide

Usage Guide

Adding a New Bourbon

  1. Tap the "+" button in the top right corner
  2. Fill in the required fields (marked with *):
    • Name
    • Proof (must be between 80-160)
    • Price
    • Purchase Date
  3. Optional fields:
    • Photo (take a new photo or choose from library)
    • Purchase Location (search nearby businesses)
    • Flavor Profile
    • Size (select from predefined options)
    • Rating (👎, 👌, or 👍)
    • Fill Level (slide to indicate remaining amount)
    • Notes
  4. Tap "Save" to add the bourbon to your collection

Managing Your Collection

  • View all bourbons in a scrollable list
  • Tap any bourbon to view its details
  • Swipe left on a bourbon to delete it
  • Use the search bar to find specific bourbons

Tracking Bourbon Lifecycle

  • Record when you open a bottle using the "Date Opened" field
  • Mark when you finish a bottle using the "Date Emptied" field
  • Use the Fill Level slider to track remaining amount
  • Note: Date tracking is not available for small sizes (50ml, 100ml, Tasting)

Tips & Tricks

Quick Location Entry: After using a location 3 times, it appears in the saved locations list (📋 icon) for quick selection.
Photo Tips:
  • Take photos in good lighting for best results
  • Include the label clearly in the frame
  • Photos are automatically saved to a "My Bourbon Collection" album
Rating System:
  • 👎 = 1 star (Not recommended)
  • 👌 = 3 stars (Good)
  • 👍 = 5 stars (Excellent)
Fill Level Tracking:
  • Use the slider to quickly update remaining amount
  • Red indicator appears when below 10%
  • Perfect for tracking shared bottles
Location Search:
  • Type at least 3 characters to search nearby businesses
  • Results show approximate distance
  • Frequently used locations are saved for quick access

Introduction

Welcome to My Bourbon Collection, your personal bourbon tracking companion! This app helps you:

  • Build and maintain your bourbon collection
  • Track your tasting experiences
  • Remember where you found each bottle
  • Monitor your collection's lifecycle

Whether you're a casual enthusiast or a serious collector, this app provides the tools you need to:

  • Document your bourbon journey
  • Make informed purchasing decisions
  • Share your experiences with others
  • Build a comprehensive collection database
