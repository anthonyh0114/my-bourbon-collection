My Bourbon Collection - User's Guide

Usage Guide

Adding a New Bourbon

  1. Tap the "+" button in the top right corner
  2. Fill in the required fields (marked with *):
    • Name
    • Proof (must be between 80-160)
    • Price
    • Purchase Date
  3. Optional fields:
    • Photo (take a new photo or choose from library)
    • Purchase Location (search nearby businesses)
    • Flavor Profile
    • Size (select from predefined options)
    • Rating (👎, 👌, or 👍)
    • Fill Level (slide to indicate remaining amount)
    • Notes
  4. Tap "Save" to add the bourbon to your collection

Managing Your Collection

  • View all bourbons in a scrollable list or grid view (toggle with the view button)
  • Tap any bourbon to view its details
  • NEW: Swipe left or right in detail view to navigate between bourbons
  • Swipe left on a bourbon in the list to delete it
  • Use the search bar to find specific bourbons

Tracking Bourbon Lifecycle

  • Record when you open a bottle using the "Date Opened" field
  • Mark when you finish a bottle using the "Date Emptied" field
  • Use the Fill Level slider to track remaining amount
  • Note: Date tracking is not available for small sizes (50ml, 100ml, Tasting)

Enhanced Navigation 🔄

Navigate through your bourbon collection with intuitive swipe gestures:

  • Swipe Right: View the previous bourbon in your collection
  • Swipe Left: View the next bourbon in your collection
  • Page Indicators: Dots at the bottom show your current position and total count
  • Smart Title: Displays current bourbon name and position (e.g., "Buffalo Trace (2 of 5)")
  • Seamless Editing: Edit and delete functions work while swiping between bourbons
  • Automatic Navigation: When deleting a bourbon, automatically shows the next available one
Pro Tip: This feature works from both the main list view and search results, making it easy to browse through filtered collections.
