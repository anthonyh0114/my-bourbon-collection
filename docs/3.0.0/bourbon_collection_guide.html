🥃 My Bourbon Collection

A practical guide to Collection, Tasting, and Hunt vaults — sharing, backup, reports, and Kiosk Mode. Tap ☰ (top left) to jump around.

📱 Welcome

My Bourbon Collection organizes your bourbon life in three vaults:

  • Collection your bottles
  • Tasting your tastings
  • Hunt availability sightings

Infinity Bottle blending is not part of the current release; if you see a placeholder, a future update will enable it.

🚀 Getting started

First launch

  1. Permissions screen — On first launch you’ll see a guided permissions screen (no surprise pop-ups). You can grant or skip: Notifications, Camera, Location, and Photos.
  2. Email registration — You may be invited to register your email for updates. It’s optional; you can Skip for Now and still use the app. If you skip, the app may remind you again after ~30 days.

Location

Location is optional. When enabled, nearby business search for purchase or availability locations uses a short-range radius so results stay local. You can always type a place manually.

Notifications

Notifications are optional. The app can send reminders and update notices only if you grant permission. If you register your email for updates, the app avoids scheduling the “monthly reminder” notification (email subscribers get updates by email instead).

🧭 Navigation, Account, and View menu

Vault banner

At the top you’ll see MBC: Collection / Tasting / Hunt Vault. The banner stays collapsed while you scroll so the list layout stays stable.

Search bar (under the banner)

Filters the current vault’s list in place (bottle names, locations, prices in Collection; tasting text in Tasting; hunt fields in Hunt). This is separate from View → Search (see below).

Bottom bar

  • Account — Status summary, Notifications & preferences, Backup & Restore, Help (opens this guide), badge unlocks (tap to share), and in-app collection sharing (username, directory visibility, identity tag, inbox) when enabled.
  • Modes — Switch vault; open Kiosk Mode and Consult the Colonel.
  • View — Context-specific actions (see next section).
  • Add (+) — Adds a bottle, tasting, or hunt depending on the active vault.

When drafts exist, you may see Complete (N) to finish “save for later” entries.

View menu (sheet)

  • Search — Opens the full Search screen (bourbon search, including flavor search where available).
  • Sort — Available in all three vaults. Options depend on the vault (see Collection / Tasting / Hunt sections).
  • Collection only: Filter (show/hide the status filter row: All / Open / Unopened / Favorites / High Value), List / Grid, and Share open bottles (QR).

📚 Collection vault

Your bottles, photos, lifecycle, notes, estimated value (for supported sizes), and open-bottle sharing.

List and grid

  • List — Compact rows, great for sorting and scanning.
  • Grid — Photo-first browsing.
  • Switch via View → List or Grid.

Sort (Collection)

From View → Sort: Name (A–Z / Z–A), Proof (low/high), Purchase date (oldest/newest), Rating (low/high, from tasting history tied to collection bottles), plus Reset to database order.

Filter

After you show the filter row via View → Filter, use All, Open, Unopened, Favorites, or High Value. Switching vaults resets the vault search field; returning to Collection with a narrow filter and no matches can look empty until you choose All.

Add or edit a bottle

  1. Tap + in Collection (or edit from detail).
  2. Core fields include name, proof, optional ABV, purchase location, purchase date, price (or gifted), size, lifecycle (date opened / emptied, fill level where applicable), review and rating text, personal notes, and photos.

Bottle sizes (picker)

Tasting, Shooter (50 ml), 100 ml, 200 ml, 375 ml, 700 ml, 750 ml, 1000 ml, 1500 ml, 1750 ml. Very small formats may limit lifecycle/fill behavior. Estimated value tracking applies to specific larger sizes the app supports (for example 375 ml, 700 ml, 750 ml, 1500 ml, and 1750 ml — not every size in the picker).

BourbonReader (wand) analyzes a clear label photo to help fill the form. Col Wood’s Professional Review generates a short review you can copy into your notes (Collection/Tasting add flow — not on Hunt).

Share open bottles

View → Share open bottles (Collection only): QR code and link for bottles that are open (opened, not emptied). Recipients open the list in the app.

🍷 Tasting vault

Log where you tasted, what was in the flight, star ratings, nose/palate/finish, and flavor tags.

Add a tasting

  1. Switch to Tasting via Modes.
  2. Tap +, pick date/location, add one or more bourbons (from your collection or as “wild” entries), rate, and add notes.

Flavor tags and Flavor Radar

Tags use categories such as Grain & Cereal, Sweet, Baking Spices, Fruit & Floral, Wood & Earth, with intensity (Subtle / Medium / Strong). They power the Flavor Radar on tasting and bottle detail and support search by flavor from the Search screen.

Sort (Tasting)

View → Sort: Name (by bourbons in the tasting), Purchase date (label in the menu; sorts by tasting date), Age (days since that tasting date), Rating (overall tasting stars; unrated last). No proof sort (a tasting has no single proof). Reset reloads from the database.

🔎 Hunt vault

Quick logs for bottles you spot in the wild — store, bar, or event — with an edit-later workflow when signal is weak.

Add or edit a hunt

The app uses one unified add/edit screen for every vault. For Hunt, several Collection-only controls are removed from the layout (not merely greyed out):

  • Col Wood’s Professional Review
  • Purchase date row and thumbs down / OK / thumbs up rating row
  • Date Opened, Date Emptied, fill level (slider and labels), and Personal Notes

You still enter bourbon name, availability location, proof/ABV as needed, price, size, gifted switch, photos, and use BourbonReader where appropriate. The hunt record stores its own date when you save.

Weak signal

Take a clear photo and save. Later, open the hunt, tap Edit, and run BourbonReader or refine the location on Wi‑Fi.

Sort (Hunt)

View → Sort: Name, Proof, Purchase date (menu label; sorts by hunt date), Age (days since that date). No star-rating sort (hunts don’t use collection rating). Reset reloads hunts from the database.

Challenges

The Hunt area can include Challenges for themed hunt sets (for example limited releases).

📤 Sharing

Open bottles (QR / link)

From Collection: View → Share open bottles. Shows currently open bottles; share QR or copy link.

In-app collection snapshot

From Account: choose a username (one-time), optionally enable Visible in Directory, and optionally add an Identity tag (a short line shown under your username to help friends recognize you). Then use Send my collection to deliver a read-only snapshot (open and unopened bottles) to a friend’s Collection inbox. This is separate from the open-bottle QR/link.

Badges (shareable)

In Account, you’ll see bottle-count badges (10, 25, 50, 100, 250, 500, 1000). When a badge is unlocked, you can tap it to share.

📝 Save for later (drafts)

If a network step times out (for example Col Wood review or label analysis), you can Save for later. When drafts exist, use Complete (N) to finish them.

💾 Data management

Open backup and reports from the Account flow (and related controls on the main Collection experience where applicable).

  • Create backup / Restore / manage backup files.
  • Insurance report (PDF) and Inventory report (CSV).

From Tasting or Hunt, choosing data backup may prompt you to switch to Collection first — that is intentional.

✨ Consult the Colonel

From Modes → Consult the Colonel: pick a mood and flavor profile (quick picks or custom text). Colonel Wood suggests a bottle from your non-empty collection with a short rationale.

While Colonel Wood thinks

Instead of a plain spinner (when it applies), the app can show a quick label reel using up to 25 random bottle photos from your collection. The reel slows down after the recommendation is ready. On the last few slowdown steps, if that bottle has a label photo saved in the app, the reel settles on that image before the loading overlay goes away.

The first moment the reel switches to your pick uses a very short cross-dissolve (about two tenths of a second). If the reel is not used—for example only one collection photo, or Reduce Motion is on in iOS Settings → Accessibility—you’ll see the usual progress indicator; when a label exists, it can fade in briefly before the result appears.

Tip: Add label photos to more bottles if you want a fuller reel and a clearer “land on the pick” moment.

🖥 Kiosk mode

For public or shared devices. Kiosk can limit editing, deletion, and some actions. Enter from Modes → Kiosk Mode.

🎯 Tips and troubleshooting

Why didn’t I get the monthly reminder notification?
The monthly reminder is intended for users who haven’t registered an email. If you’ve registered, updates are handled by email and the app avoids scheduling that monthly notification. Also confirm notifications are enabled in iOS Settings.
Sort does nothing in Tasting or Hunt.
Use View → Sort. Each vault sorts its own list; Reset reloads from the database.
Why doesn’t Hunt show purchase date or bottle lifecycle?
Hunt is a sighting log, not a bottle in your collection. Those fields are hidden on Hunt add/edit so the form matches how hunts are stored.
Where is Help?
Open Account, then Help & User's Guide. The guide is bundled with the app for your installed version (works offline). The same file is published on GitHub under docs/3.0.0/ for the web.
Weak signal at a bar or store?
Create a Hunt with a good photo; edit later on stronger signal.

🔒 Privacy (summary)

  • Location is optional.
  • Your data stays yours until you explicitly share (QR/link or in-app send).
  • Notifications are opt-in. Email registration is optional; if you register, some in-app reminders may be reduced (for example the monthly reminder).
  • Notifications, email, and analytics follow the choices you make in the app and system Settings.
  • You must be of legal drinking age in your region.

For full legal text, use the policies linked or shown inside the app where provided.
