Introduction

My Bourbon Collection is an iOS application designed to help bourbon enthusiasts track and manage their collection. Whether you're a casual collector or a serious connoisseur, this app provides an easy way to catalog your bottles, record tasting notes, and keep track of your ratings.

Installation

To install My Bourbon Collection:

  1. Clone the repository from GitHub
  2. Open the project in Xcode 15.0 or later
  3. Build and run the project on your iOS device or simulator (iOS 17.0 or later required)

Features

  • Collection Management
    • Add new bourbons to your collection
    • Edit existing entries
    • Delete bottles from your collection
    • View your collection in list or grid format
  • Bottle Information
    • Name and brand
    • Bottle size (50ml, 100ml, 375ml, 750ml, 1500ml, 1750ml)
    • Proof/ABV
    • Age statement
    • Price
    • Purchase date
    • Bottle photo
  • Tasting Notes
    • Record detailed tasting notes
    • Rate bourbons on a 5-star scale
    • Track your preferences over time
  • View Options
    • Switch between list and grid views
    • Sort by name, rating, or purchase date
    • Search your collection

Usage Guide

Adding a New Bourbon

  1. Tap the "+" button in the top right corner
  2. Fill in the required information:
    • Name (required)
    • Size (required)
    • Proof/ABV (required)
    • Age statement (optional)
    • Price (optional)
    • Purchase date (optional)
    • Photo (optional)
  3. Tap "Save" to add the bourbon to your collection

Editing a Bourbon

  1. Select a bourbon from your collection
  2. Tap the "Edit" button
  3. Modify any information as needed
  4. Tap "Save" to update the entry

Adding Tasting Notes

  1. Select a bourbon from your collection
  2. Tap "Add Tasting Notes"
  3. Enter your notes in the text field
  4. Rate the bourbon using the star rating system
  5. Tap "Save" to record your notes

Viewing Your Collection

  • Use the segmented control to switch between list and grid views
  • Tap the sort button to change the sorting order
  • Use the search bar to find specific bourbons
  • Tap on any bourbon to view its details

Tips & Tricks

  • Take clear, well-lit photos of your bottles for better visual organization
  • Use consistent naming conventions for easier searching
  • Regularly update your tasting notes to track how your preferences evolve
  • Use the search function to quickly find specific bottles in large collections
  • Take advantage of the grid view for a visual overview of your collection
Support: If you encounter any issues or have suggestions for improvements, please open an issue on the GitHub repository.
