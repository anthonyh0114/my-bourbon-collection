📱 Welcome to Your Bourbon Collection

My Bourbon Collection is your personal digital companion for managing and tracking your bourbon collection, tastings, and discovering new releases. This guide will walk you through all the features and help you get the most out of your bourbon journey.

🚀 Getting Started

First Launch Experience

When you first open the app, you'll experience a smooth onboarding flow:

  1. Launch Screen - Beautiful bourbon-themed welcome
  2. Permissions Screen - Request access to camera, location, photo library, and push notifications
  3. Email Registration - Stay in the loop with bourbon news and events

📧 Email Registration - "Stay in the Loop"

Why Register?

  • Get notified about app updates and new features
  • Receive alerts about bourbon tastings in your area
  • Learn about new bourbon releases from your favorite distilleries
  • Monthly reminders and exclusive content

Registration Options:

  • Enter Email - Full access to all notifications
  • Skip for Now - You can always register later
  • Manage Preferences - Control what you receive

Monthly Reminders:

  • If you skip registration, we'll gently remind you every 30 days
  • You can register anytime from the main app

🔔 Push Notifications

  • The app will request permission to send you push notifications
  • This allows you to receive timely alerts even when the app is closed
  • You can change this permission anytime in iOS Settings

📚 Managing Your Collection

Adding Bourbons

  1. Tap the + button
  2. Fill in bourbon details:
    • Name - Bourbon brand and expression
    • Proof - Alcohol content
    • Age - Years aged
    • Purchase Date - When you acquired it
    • Price - How much you paid
    • Notes - Personal observations
    • Photos - Add bottle and label images

Organizing Your Collection

Sort Options

  • Name (A-Z or Z-A)
  • Proof (Low to High or High to Low)
  • Date (Oldest to Newest or Newest to Oldest)
  • Age (Youngest to Oldest or Oldest to Youngest)
  • Rating (Low to High or High to High)

View Options

  • List View - Compact, detailed information
  • Grid View - Visual, photo-focused layout

Editing and Updating

  • Tap any bourbon to view details
  • Edit button to modify information
  • Delete option for removing from collection

🍷 Tasting Notes & Reviews

Recording Tastings

  1. Add Tasting from bourbon detail view
  2. Rate your experience (1-5 stars)
  3. Add Notes:
    • Nose (aroma)
    • Palate (taste)
    • Finish (aftertaste)
    • Overall impressions

📍 Location Features

Local Bourbon Discovery

  • Find Events - Discover tastings and releases near you
  • Radius Settings - Adjust search area (default: 50 miles)
  • Privacy Focused - Only city/state, no exact coordinates

🆘 Help & Support

Getting Help

Common Questions

Q: How do I change my notification preferences?
A: Tap the bell icon (🔔) anywhere in the app to access "Manage All Preferences"
Q: Can I use the app without sharing my location?
A: Yes! Location is optional. You can decline and still enjoy all other features.
Q: How do I backup my collection?
A: Tap the backup button (🔄) in the bottom navigation, then "Create Backup"
Q: What if I want to stop receiving notifications?
A: Go to any bell icon (🔔) and select "Unsubscribe from All" or adjust individual preferences.
Q: How do I delete my account?
A: Go to any bell icon (🔔) and select "Delete My Account" (⚠️ This action cannot be undone)
Q: What happens after I delete my account?
A: Your account data is completely removed, and you'll see a "Register New Account" option to start fresh
Q: Can I re-register after unsubscribing?
A: Yes! Use the "Re-enter Email" button to resubscribe immediately, or wait for the monthly reminder
