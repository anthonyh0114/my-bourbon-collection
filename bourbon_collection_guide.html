Your personal digital companion for managing and tracking your bourbon collection, tastings, and discovering new releases

📱 Welcome to Your Bourbon Collection

My Bourbon Collection is your personal digital companion for managing and tracking your bourbon collection, tastings, and discovering new releases. This guide will walk you through all the features and help you get the most out of your bourbon journey.

🏠 Main App Interface

🔄 Three Vaults (Unified Interface)

The app uses a unified interface with three vaults—Collection, Tastings, and Hunt. Switch vaults by tapping Modes in the bottom toolbar and choosing a vault. The header and list content update for the active vault.

  • Collection – Your owned bottles, with ratings, stats, filters, list/grid view, and estimated value
  • Tastings – Your tasting experiences, notes, and ratings
  • Hunt – Bottles you spot in the wild (stores, bars, events), with photos, prices, and open/closed status

The + button label changes by vault: "Add Bourbon," "Add Tasting," or "Add Hunt."

Search Bar (Vault Search)

Directly under the banner, a search bar filters the current vault only (Collection, Tastings, or Hunt). Tap × to clear; keyboard dismisses on scroll, tap elsewhere, or clear.

Bottom Toolbar

A single floating bar at the bottom of the screen (rounded, translucent) shows these buttons left to right:

  • Account (person icon) – Opens the Account & Status sheet. At the top it shows status (in Collection: bottle count and value; in Tasting/Hunt: a message to switch to Collection for stats). Two options: Notifications & preferences and Backup & Restore.
  • Modes (cabinet icon) – Opens the Modes & Tools sheet with two sections: Switch Vault (choose Collection, Tasting, or Hunt) and Tools (Kiosk Mode, Consult the Colonel).
  • View (filter/status icon) – Opens the View options sheet: Search, Filter, Sort; in Collection mode only, also List View / Grid View and Share Open Bottles.
  • Help (?) – Opens this User's Guide. This button is only visible when you are in Collection vault. In Tasting or Hunt, the Help button is hidden; switch to Collection via Modes to see it.
  • Add (+) – Adds an item for the current vault: Add Bourbon (Collection), Add Tasting (Tasting), or Add Hunt (Hunt).

View Options Sheet

Tapping View opens a sheet titled View options with: Search, Filter, Sort. When you are in Collection vault, the sheet also includes List View / Grid View and Share Open Bottles.

Modes & Tools Sheet

Tapping Modes opens a sheet titled Modes & Tools. Section Switch Vault lets you choose Collection, Tasting, or Hunt (current vault has a checkmark). Section Tools has Kiosk Mode and Consult the Colonel.

📚 Managing Your Collection

Adding Bourbons

  1. Switch to Collection vault via Modes if needed
  2. Tap the + button (title shows "Add Bourbon")
  3. Fill in bourbon details:
    • Name * - Bourbon brand and expression (required)
    • Proof * - Alcohol content (required)
    • ABV - Alcohol by volume percentage
    • 🎯 Col Wood's Professional Review - Get professional bourbon reviews from Colonel Wood
    • Purchase Location - Where you bought it (2-city-block search)
    • Review and Rating - Your tasting notes and ratings
    • Price * - How much you paid (required)
    • Size - Bottle size (750ml, 1L, etc.)
    • Purchase Date - When you acquired it
    • Date Opened - When you first opened it
    • Date Emptied - When you finished it
    • Rating - Your personal rating (1-5 stars)
    • Fill Level - How much is left in the bottle
    • Notes - Personal observations and memories
    • Photos - Add bottle and label images

🎯 New: Col Wood's Professional Review System

Col Wood's Bourbon Reviews: Get professional-quality bourbon descriptions generated by the all knowing Col Wood (our version of the Wizard of Oz). Simply enter a bourbon name and tap the "Col Wood's Professional Review" button to receive a detailed, 250-character review that you can copy to the Review and Rating field.

How It Works:

  1. Enter the bourbon name in the Name field
  2. Tap the "Col Wood's Professional Review" button
  3. Choose to copy the Col's review to your Review and Rating field or just close the popup

Benefits: Professional-quality descriptions, complete sentences, 250 character limits, and full user control over when and how to use Col Wood's reviews.

✨ Consult the Colonel - Personalized Recommendations

Get Expert Bourbon Recommendations

Consult the Colonel provides personalized bourbon recommendations from your own collection based on your current mood and flavor preferences. Colonel Wood, our Southern gentleman bourbon expert, will study your collection and suggest the perfect bottle for any occasion.

How to Use

  1. Tap Modes in the bottom toolbar to open the Modes & Tools sheet, then under Tools tap Consult the Colonel
  2. Enter your mood or occasion:
    • Quick Selection: Tap pre-selected buttons like [Relaxing], [Celebrating], [Social Gathering], [Quiet Evening], or [Special Occasion]
    • Custom Input: Type your own mood description
    • Examples: "Relaxing evening by the fireplace", "Celebrating with friends", "Trying something bold"
  3. Describe your desired flavor profile:
    • Quick Selection: Tap pre-selected buttons like [Spicy], [Sweet], [Oak Forward], [Smooth], [Bold], or [Vanilla]
    • Custom Input: Type your own flavor preferences
    • Examples: "Sweet, vanilla-forward, and easy-drinking", "Spicy and complex", "Smooth and mellow"
    • Tip: You can combine multiple quick selections or mix them with custom text
  4. Tap "Consult the Colonel" and wait for Colonel Wood's recommendation

What You'll Get

Colonel Wood will:

  • Review your collection (only considers bottles that aren't empty)
  • Match your mood and preferences to the perfect bourbon
  • Provide a personalized rating (1-5 stars) with his charming review
  • Explain why this bourbon fits your needs

Features

  • Smart Selection - Only recommends from your available (non-empty) bottles
  • Personalized Reviews - Concise, charming reviews (425 characters) in Colonel Wood's Southern gentleman voice
  • Mood Matching - Considers your occasion and desired experience
  • Flavor Focus - Matches your preferred taste profile
  • Quick Access - Tap the recommendation to view full bourbon details
  • Try Again - Get a new recommendation anytime

Tips for Best Results

  • Be specific about your mood and occasion for better matches
  • Describe flavors you're craving (sweet, spicy, smooth, bold, etc.)
  • Try different combinations - same bourbon can fit different moods
  • View details to see why Colonel Wood recommended it
  • Experiment with different mood/flavor combinations

Example Recommendations

Scenario 1: Quiet Evening

  • Mood: "Relaxing evening by the fireplace"
  • Flavor: "Sweet, vanilla-forward, and easy-drinking"
  • Result: Smooth, lower-proof bourbon perfect for sipping

Scenario 2: Special Occasion

Common Questions

Q: How do I change my notification preferences?
A: Tap the Account button in the bottom toolbar, then tap Notifications & preferences in the Account & Status sheet.
Q: Why don't I see the Help button?
A: The Help button (?) is only shown in the bottom bar when you are in Collection vault. In Tasting or Hunt, the bar does not show Help. Switch to Collection via the Modes button (Modes & Tools → Switch Vault → Collection) to open the User's Guide.
Q: Can I use the app without sharing my location?
A: Yes! Location is optional. You can decline and still enjoy all other features.
Q: How do I backup my collection?
A: Tap the Account button in the bottom toolbar, then tap Backup & Restore in the Account & Status sheet. From there choose "Create Backup". The app will create both a local backup and attempt to backup to your network location (if available). If network backup isn't available, you can use the "Share Backup File" option to manually save it to your network drive via the Files app.
Q: What if I want to stop receiving notifications?
A: Tap Account in the bottom toolbar, then tap Notifications & preferences. From there you can unsubscribe from all or adjust individual preferences.
Q: How do I delete my account?
A: Tap Account in the bottom toolbar, then tap Notifications & preferences. Account deletion is available there (⚠️ cannot be undone).
Q: What happens after I delete my account?
A: Your account data is completely removed, and you'll see a "Register New Account" option to start fresh
Q: Can I re-register after unsubscribing?
A: Yes! Use the "Change Email" button to edit your email, or "Start Over" to clear fields and re-enter
Q: Why do I need to enter my email twice during registration?
A: This prevents typos and ensures you receive important notifications. Both fields must match exactly.
Q: How do I get the Col's bourbon reviews?
A: Enter a bourbon name in the Name field, then tap the "Col Wood's Professional Review" button. Choose whether to copy the review to your Review and Rating field or just close the popup.
Q: Why are location search results more focused now?
A: We've implemented a 2-city-block radius (400 meters) to ensure all results are within walking distance and eliminate businesses that are miles away.
Q: What happened to the "Flavor Profile" field?
A: We've renamed it to "Review and Rating" to better reflect its purpose and accommodate both the Col's reviews and your personal tasting notes.
Q: How does the location caching work?
A: Location search results are cached for 30 minutes to provide consistent, reliable listings and reduce unnecessary API calls.
Q: How does the vault / mode system work?
A: Collection, Tasting, and Hunt are three vaults on one screen. Tap Modes in the bottom toolbar to open the Modes & Tools sheet. Under Switch Vault, tap Collection, Tasting, or Hunt. The header and list update for the active vault. The Help button (?) only appears in the bar when you are in Collection; in Tasting or Hunt, switch to Collection via Modes to open the User's Guide.
Q: How do I add items in different modes?
A: The + button dynamically changes its title based on the current mode: "Add Bourbon" in Collection mode, "Add Tasting" in Tastings mode, and "Add Hunt" in Hunt mode. Tap the + button to add items to the active mode.
Q: What is BourbonReader?
A: BourbonReader is an AI-powered label scanner that extracts comprehensive information from bottle label images, including brand, proof, ABV, mashbill, bottling info, and more. It's available in Add Bourbon, Add Tasting, and Add Hunt screens.
Q: How do I use BourbonReader?
A: Take or select a photo of the bottle label, then tap the "BourbonReader" button. The AI will analyze the image and automatically populate fields with extracted information. Review and edit as needed.
Q: What do the Open/Closed badges mean on Hunt items?
A: Each hunt displays a status badge: 🟢 OPEN (green) means the hunt is still active, and ⚫ CLOSED (gray) means the hunt has been completed, typically after adding to collection.
Q: What happens when I add a Hunt to Collection?
A: When you tap "Add to Collection" from Hunt Details, you'll be prompted to either "Mark as Closed" or "Keep Open". The bourbon name, store, price, proof, and ABV are automatically migrated to the Add Bourbon screen.
Q: Can I delete multiple backup files at once?
A: Yes! When viewing backups, you can select multiple files and delete them all at once using the multi-select deletion feature.
