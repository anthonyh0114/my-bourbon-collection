🚀 Getting Started

First launch, registration, and setup

First Launch

  1. Launch Screen — Bourbon-themed welcome experience
  2. Permissions — Camera, location, photo library, and push notifications
  3. Email Registration — Optional: stay in the loop with bourbon news and events

Email Registration

Why Register?

  • App updates and new feature announcements
  • Alerts about bourbon tastings in your area
  • New release notifications from favorite distilleries
  • Monthly reminders and exclusive content

Registration Process

  • Double Email Entry — Enter your email twice to prevent typos; both fields must match
  • Real-time Validation — Visual feedback when emails match
  • Change Email — Edit your address if needed
  • Start Over — Clear both fields for fresh input
  • Skip for Now — Register anytime later; app reminds you every 30 days

Push notification permission is requested at first launch. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings.

Vault Search Bar

Located directly under the banner — filters the current vault only. Tap × to clear.

📚 Managing Your Collection

Adding, organizing, and editing bottles

Adding a Bourbon

  1. Switch to Collection vault via Modes if needed
  2. Tap the + button ("Add Bourbon")
  3. Fill in details — required: Name, Proof, and Price
  4. Optionally get a review from Col Wood's Professional Review
  5. Save your entry

All Available Fields

  • Name, Proof, Price — Required
  • ABV, Purchase Location, Size, Purchase Date, Date Opened, Date Emptied
  • Review and Rating — Tasting notes; can be auto-filled by Col Wood
  • Rating — Personal 1–5 star rating
  • Fill Level, Notes, Photos

🎯 Col Wood's Professional Review

  1. Enter the bourbon name in the Name field
  2. Tap "Col Wood's Professional Review"
  3. Choose to copy the review into the Review & Rating field, or close

Reviews are ~250 characters, professional-quality, in Col Wood's Southern gentleman voice.

BourbonReader — Colonel Wood's wizardry Label Scanner

Available in Add Bourbon, Add Tasting, and Add Hunt. Photograph a bottle label, tap BourbonReader, and Colonel Wood's wizardry extracts brand, proof, ABV, mashbill, and bottling info — then populates the form fields automatically.

Sort & View Options

  • Sort by: Name, Proof, Date, or Rating (ascending or descending)
  • List View — Compact with detailed information
  • Grid View — Visual, photo-focused layout

✨ Consult the Colonel

Personalized recommendations from your collection

Consult the Colonel analyzes your collection and suggests the perfect bottle based on your mood and flavor preferences. Access via Modes → Tools → Consult the Colonel.

How to Use

  1. Tap Modes → Consult the Colonel
  2. Select or type your mood / occasion — quick picks: Relaxing, Celebrating, Social Gathering, Quiet Evening, Special Occasion
  3. Select or type your desired flavor profile — quick picks: Spicy, Sweet, Oak Forward, Smooth, Bold, Vanilla
  4. Tap "Consult the Colonel" to receive your recommendation

What You Get

  • Only considers non-empty bottles in your collection
  • Personalized 1–5 star rating with a ~425-character review in Col Wood's voice
  • Explains why this bourbon fits your mood and flavor request
  • Tap the recommendation to jump to full bottle details
  • Try Again for a fresh recommendation anytime

Example — Quiet Evening

Mood: "Relaxing by the fireplace" + Flavor: "Sweet, vanilla-forward" → Smooth, lower-proof sipping bourbon

Example — Special Occasion

Mood: "Celebrating a milestone" + Flavor: "Complex, bold, memorable" → Premium high-proof selection

🍷 Tasting Notes & Reviews

Recording and tracking your tasting experiences

Recording a Tasting

  1. Switch to Tastings vault, or tap Add Tasting from a bourbon's detail view
  2. Rate your experience (1–5 stars)
  3. Add notes for Nose (aroma), Palate (taste), Finish (aftertaste), and overall impressions
  4. Save your tasting entry

Tasting History

  • Track how your palate evolves over time
  • Compare different expressions of the same bourbon
  • Search tastings using the vault search bar

Hunt Vault

Log bottles you spot in stores, bars, and events before you decide to buy.

  • 🟢 OPEN — Hunt is still active
  • ⚫ CLOSED — Hunt completed (e.g., added to collection)
  • Tapping "Add to Collection" from Hunt Details prompts Mark as Closed or Keep Open, and migrates bourbon name, store, price, proof, and ABV automatically

📍 Location Features

Smart local search for stores and events

2-City-Block Radius (400m)

All location results are within walking distance. Results are cached for 30 minutes for consistent, reliable listings.

What Location Enables

  • Purchase Location Search — Find nearby liquor stores, bars, and restaurants when logging a bottle
  • Local Events — Discover bourbon tastings near you
  • Auto City/State Detection — App detects your general area for relevant notifications

Privacy Notes

  • Location is completely optional — app works fully without it
  • Only city/state level is used — exact GPS coordinates are never stored
  • Revoke permission anytime in iOS Settings
  • Powered by Apple Location Services and Google Places API

🔔 Notifications & Account

Managing alerts, emails, and account settings

Access from anywhere: tap Account in the bottom toolbar → Notifications & preferences.

Notification Types

  • App Updates, Bourbon Tastings, Bourbon Releases, Monthly Reminders
  • Push Notifications — In-app alerts
  • Email Notifications — Email updates

Controls

  • Individual Toggles — Turn specific types on or off
  • Unsubscribe All / Resubscribe — Bulk control anytime

Account Management

  • View and update your registered email
  • See registration date and last login
  • Update your current location
  • Delete Account — Permanent removal; cannot be undone

⚠️ Account deletion is permanent. All data removed within 30 days. You can register again with a new account.

💾 Data & Backup

Backing up, restoring, and exporting your collection

Access via Account (bottom toolbar) → Backup & Restore.

Backup Options

  • Create Backup — Saves locally and attempts network backup to SMB share. If unavailable, share manually via Files app.
  • Restore from Backup — Recover your collection from any saved backup
  • View Backups — See file names, sizes, dates; multi-select deletion to clean up old files

Report Generation

  • Insurance Report (PDF) — Document your collection's value
  • Inventory Report (CSV) — Spreadsheet-friendly full export

Share via Messages, email, AirDrop, or save to Files.
