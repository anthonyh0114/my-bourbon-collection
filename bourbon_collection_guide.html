Introduction

My Bourbon Collection is an iOS application designed to help bourbon enthusiasts track and manage their collection. Whether you're a casual collector or a serious connoisseur, this app provides an easy way to catalog your bottles, record tasting notes, and keep track of your ratings. Created by Col Wood, a bourbon enthusiast and developer, this app combines professional software design with a passion for fine spirits.

Installation

To install My Bourbon Collection:

  1. Open the App Store on your iOS device
  2. Search for "My Bourbon Collection" or visit the direct link: App Store Link
  3. Tap "Get" or the price ($1.99) to purchase the app
  4. Authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, or your Apple ID password
  5. Wait for the download to complete
  6. Once installed, tap the app icon to launch
Requirements:
  • iOS 18.4 or later
  • iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro
  • Approximately 24.9 MB of storage space
Family Sharing: Up to six family members can use this app with Family Sharing enabled.

Features

  • Collection Categories
    • Inventory - Track your current bottle collection
    • Tastings - Record your tasting experiences
    • Infinity - Coming soon! Track your infinity bottle collection
  • Collection Management
    • Add new bourbons to your collection
    • Add tasting experiences
    • Edit existing entries
    • Delete bottles from your collection
    • View your collection in list or grid format
  • Bottle Information
    • Name and brand
    • Bottle size (Shooter - 50ml, 100ml, Tasting, 375ml, 750ml, 1500ml, 1750ml)
    • Proof (required) - ABV is automatically calculated as Proof ÷ 2
    • Age statement
    • Price
    • Purchase date (required)
    • Date opened (optional)
    • Date emptied (optional)
    • Bottle photo
  • Rating System
    • Simple 3-level rating system (👎, 👌, 👍)
    • Quick visual feedback on bottle quality
  • View Options
    • Toggle between list and grid views using the layout button
    • Sort by multiple criteria:
      • Name (A-Z or Z-A)
      • Proof (Low to High or High to Low)
      • Age (Youngest to Oldest or Oldest to Youngest)
      • Purchase Date (Oldest to Newest or Newest to Oldest)
      • Rating (Low to High or High to Low)
    • Search your collection
    • Pinch to zoom in grid view

Usage Guide

Adding a New Bourbon to Inventory

  1. Select "Inventory" from the category options
  2. Tap the "+" floating action button in the bottom right corner
  3. Fill in the required information:
    • Name (required)
    • Proof (required) - Enter the proof value; ABV will be automatically calculated
    • Purchase date (required)
    • Size (optional) - Choose from standard bottle sizes
    • Age statement (optional)
    • Price (optional)
    • Date opened (optional)
    • Date emptied (optional)
    • Photo (optional)
  4. Tap "Save" to add the bourbon to your inventory

Adding a Tasting Experience

  1. Select "Tastings" from the category options
  2. Tap the "+" floating action button in the bottom right corner
  3. Fill in the required information:
    • Name (required)
    • Proof (required) - Enter the proof value; ABV will be automatically calculated
    • Tasting date (required)
    • Size (optional) - Choose from standard bottle sizes
    • Age statement (optional)
    • Price (optional)
    • Photo (optional)
  4. Tap "Save" to add the tasting to your collection

Editing a Bourbon or Tasting

  1. Select a bourbon or tasting from your collection
  2. Tap the "Edit" button
  3. Modify any information as needed
  4. Tap "Save" to update the entry

Rating a Bourbon

  1. Select a bourbon from your collection
  2. Choose your rating:
    • 👎 - Not recommended
    • 👌 - Good
    • 👍 - Excellent
  3. The rating will be saved automatically

Viewing Your Collection

  • Use the grid/list toggle button to switch between views
  • In grid view, use pinch gestures to zoom in/out
  • Tap the sort button to change the sorting order
  • Use the search button to find specific bourbons
  • Tap on any bourbon to view its details

Tips & Tricks

  • Take clear, well-lit photos of your bottles for better visual organization
  • Use consistent naming conventions for easier searching
  • Use the grid view for a visual overview of your collection
  • Take advantage of the pinch-to-zoom feature in grid view for better visibility
  • Use the sort function to organize your collection by different criteria
  • The help button (?) in the bottom left corner provides quick access to this guide
  • When entering proof, the ABV will be automatically calculated (Proof ÷ 2)
  • Don't forget to set the purchase date for inventory items - it's required and helps track your collection's history
  • Track your bottles' lifecycle by recording when you open and empty them
  • Use the Tastings category to keep a record of your bourbon sampling experiences
  • Stay tuned for the Infinity category, coming soon!
Support: If you encounter any issues or have suggestions for improvements, please visit the App Store page and tap "App Support" to contact the developer.
