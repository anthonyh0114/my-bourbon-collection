📱 Welcome to Your Bourbon Collection

My Bourbon Collection is your personal digital companion for managing and tracking your bourbon collection, tastings, and discovering new releases. This guide will walk you through all the features and help you get the most out of your bourbon journey.

🚀 Getting Started

First Launch Experience

When you first open the app, you'll experience a smooth onboarding flow:

  1. Launch Screen - Beautiful bourbon-themed welcome
  2. Permissions Screen - Request access to camera, location, photo library, and push notifications
  3. Email Registration - Stay in the loop with bourbon news and events

📤 Share Open Bottles (QR / Link)

Share your current open bottles with friends so they can see what you're pouring.

How to Use

  1. In Collection mode, tap the QR icon in the tools bar.
  2. View your list of open bottles (bottles with a "Date Opened" set).
  3. Show the QR code for others to scan, or tap Copy link to send the link (e.g. in Messages or Notes).

Anyone who scans the QR code or opens the link will see the same list in the app. When they close the list, they return to the main app.

📚 Managing Your Collection

Adding Bourbons

  1. Tap the + button
  2. Fill in bourbon details:
    • Name * - Bourbon brand and expression (required)
    • Proof * - Alcohol content (required)
    • ABV - Alcohol by volume percentage
    • 🎯 Col Wood's Professional Review - Get professional bourbon reviews from Colonel Wood
    • Purchase Location - Where you bought it (2-city-block search)
    • Review and Rating - Your tasting notes and ratings
    • Price * - How much you paid (required)
    • Size - Bottle size (e.g. 50 ml, 100 ml, 200 ml, 375 ml, 700 ml, 750 ml, 1000 ml, 1500 ml, 1750 ml)
    • Purchase Date - When you acquired it
    • Date Opened - When you first opened it
    • Date Emptied - When you finished it
    • Rating - Your personal rating (1-5 stars)
    • Fill Level - How much is left in the bottle
    • Notes - Personal observations and memories
    • Photos - Add bottle and label images

🎯 Col Wood's Professional Review System

Col Wood's Bourbon Reviews: Get professional-quality bourbon descriptions generated by Colonel Wood. Enter a bourbon name and tap "Col Wood's Professional Review" to receive a detailed, 250-character review that you can copy to the Review and Rating field.

How It Works:

  1. Enter the bourbon name in the Name field
  2. Tap the "Col Wood's Professional Review" button
  3. Choose to copy the Col's review to your Review and Rating field or just close the popup

✨ Consult the Colonel - Personalized Recommendations

Get Expert Bourbon Recommendations

Consult the Colonel provides personalized bourbon recommendations from your own collection based on your current mood and flavor preferences. Colonel Wood will study your collection and suggest the perfect bottle for any occasion.

How to Use

  1. Tap the sparkles button (✨) in the bottom navigation bar
  2. Choose your mood - Tap one of the quick-select options (Relaxing, Celebrating, Social Gathering, Quiet Evening, Special Occasion) or type your own in the mood field
  3. Choose your desired flavor profile - Tap one of the options (Spicy, Sweet, Oak Forward, Smooth, Bold, Vanilla) or type your own
  4. Tap "Consult the Colonel" and wait for his recommendation

All mood and flavor quick-select buttons are shown in a vertical list so you can see every option without swiping.

What You'll Get

Colonel Wood will:

  • Review your collection (only considers bottles that aren't empty)
  • Match your mood and preferences to the perfect bourbon
  • Provide a personalized rating (1-5 stars) with his charming review
  • Explain why this bourbon fits your needs

Features

  • Smart Selection - Only recommends from your available (non-empty) bottles
  • Personalized Reviews - Concise, charming reviews in Colonel Wood's Southern gentleman voice
  • Mood Matching - Considers your occasion and desired experience
  • Flavor Focus - Matches your preferred taste profile
  • Quick Access - Tap the recommendation to view full bourbon details
  • Try Again - Get a new recommendation anytime

🍷 Tasting Notes, Flavor Tags & Reviews

Recording Tastings

  1. Add Tasting from the Tasting tab or from a bourbon's detail view
  2. Select one or more bourbons from your collection for this tasting
  3. Edit flavor tags - Tap "Edit tags" to pick flavor tags for each bourbon (optional)
  4. Rate your experience (1-5 stars)
  5. Add Notes: Nose, Palate, Finish, and overall impressions

Flavor Tags

When adding or editing a tasting, you can tag each bourbon with flavor tags for quick, consistent notes:

  • Categories: Grain & Cereal, Sweet, Baking Spices, Fruit & Floral, Wood & Earth
  • Tags include options like Sweet Corn, Toasted Malt, Crème Brûlée, Maple Syrup, Cinnamon, Stone Fruit, Dark Cherry, Toasted Oak, Old Leather, Tobacco, and more
  • Intensity - Each tag can be Subtle, Medium, or Strong (tap or long-press to cycle)
  • Smart suggestions - For certain bourbon names (e.g. wheated, high rye), the app suggests relevant tags at the top of the list

Flavor tags power the Flavor Radar chart and Search by flavor so you can find bourbons by taste profile.

Flavor Radar Chart

On tasting details and bourbon details, a Flavor Radar chart shows how the selected tags map across the five categories (Grain & Cereal, Sweet, Baking Spices, Fruit & Floral, Wood & Earth). Labels are grouped for clarity: Grain & Cereal at the top, Wood & Earth and Fruit & Floral on the left, Sweet and Baking Spices on the right.

Tasting History

  • Track how your palate evolves
  • Compare different expressions
  • Remember your favorites
  • Search by flavor in the Search screen

Save for Later (Drafts)

If you're adding a bourbon or a tasting and the app hits a network timeout (e.g. Col Wood review or label analysis), you'll see options to Save for Later, Retry, or Cancel. Choosing Save for Later stores your current form and image as a draft. Later, tap Complete (N) in the collection bar to open your saved drafts and finish submitting.

Common Questions

Q: How do I change my notification preferences?
A: Tap the bell icon (🔔) anywhere in the app, or go to Settings → Notifications → "Manage All Preferences"
Q: Can I use the app without sharing my location?
A: Yes! Location is optional. You can decline and still enjoy all other features.
Q: How do I backup my collection?
A: Tap the backup button (🔄) in the bottom navigation, then "Create Backup"
Q: What if I want to stop receiving notifications?
A: Go to any bell icon (🔔) and select "Unsubscribe from All" or adjust individual preferences.
Q: How do I delete my account?
A: Go to any bell icon (🔔) and select "Delete My Account" (⚠️ This action cannot be undone)
Q: What happens after I delete my account?
A: Your account data is completely removed, and you'll see a "Register New Account" option to start fresh
Q: Can I re-register after unsubscribing?
A: Yes! Use the "Change Email" button to edit your email, or "Start Over" to clear fields and re-enter
Q: Why do I need to enter my email twice during registration?
A: This prevents typos and ensures you receive important notifications. Both fields must match exactly.
Q: How do I get the Col's bourbon reviews?
A: Enter a bourbon name in the Name field, then tap the "Col Wood's Professional Review" button. Choose whether to copy the review to your Review and Rating field or just close the popup.
Q: Why are location search results more focused now?
A: We use a 2-city-block radius (400 meters) so all results are within walking distance and distant businesses are excluded.
Q: What are flavor tags and how do I use them?
A: When adding or editing a tasting, tap "Edit tags" for each bourbon. Pick from categories (Grain & Cereal, Sweet, Baking Spices, Fruit & Floral, Wood & Earth) and set intensity (Subtle/Medium/Strong). Tags feed the Flavor Radar and Search by flavor.
Q: How do I share my open bottles with friends?
A: In Collection mode, tap the QR icon in the tools bar. Show the QR code for others to scan, or tap "Copy link" and send the link. Opening the link shows your open bottles list in the app.
Q: I got "Save for Later" when adding a bourbon or tasting. What is that?
A: If the app hits a network timeout (e.g. Col Wood or label analysis), you can save your current form as a draft. Tap "Complete (N)" in the collection bar later to open your drafts and finish.
Q: What bottle sizes can I choose?
A: You can select Tasting, Shooter (50 ml), 100 ml, 200 ml, 375 ml, 700 ml, 750 ml, 1000 ml, 1500 ml, and 1750 ml. Market value is available for 375 ml and larger sizes where supported.
Q: How does the location caching work?
A: Location search results are cached for 30 minutes to provide consistent, reliable listings and reduce unnecessary API calls.
