Privacy Policy for My Bourbon Collection

Last Updated: [Current Date]

Introduction

My Bourbon Collection ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("App").

Information We Collect

My Bourbon Collection is designed to be a personal collection management tool. We collect the following information:

  • Photos: You can take or select photos of your bourbon bottles. These photos are stored locally on your device.
  • Location Data: The app uses your device's location services to record where you purchased your bourbon. This information is stored locally on your device.
  • Collection Data: Information about your bourbon collection, including names, proof, purchase dates, prices, and tasting notes. This data is stored locally on your device.

How We Use Your Information

All information collected by the app is used solely for the purpose of managing your personal bourbon collection. We do not:

  • Share your data with third parties
  • Use your data for advertising
  • Track your usage patterns
  • Collect analytics data

Data Storage

All your data is stored locally on your device. We do not maintain any servers or cloud storage for your information. This means:

  • Your data is not backed up to our servers
  • We cannot access your data
  • If you delete the app, your data will be deleted

Required Permissions

The app requires the following permissions to function properly:

  • Camera: To take photos of your bourbon bottles
  • Photo Library: To select existing photos of your bourbon bottles
  • Location Services: To record purchase locations

Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • You can delete any information you've entered
  • You can revoke camera and location permissions at any time through your device settings
  • You can delete the app at any time, which will remove all stored data

Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for use by children under the age of 21. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 21.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
